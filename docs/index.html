FAQ

Questions

IS IT FREE?

Yes, all of it.

WHAT DO I NEED?

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Each program is self-contained, so you don't need to install .NET or anything else.

IS THERE A MAC VERSION?

Retro Radio runs on Apple Silicon Macs (M1 and newer): download it from the Retro Radio card. It isn't signed with a paid Apple developer account, so the first time open Terminal and run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Retro Radio.app", then open it normally. The other programs are Windows-only for now.

HOW DO SPOTIFY & SOUNDCLOUD WORK?

Open SETTINGS → MUSIC SERVICE on the radio and follow the steps to add your own free developer app key, then sign in. Spotify plays through your Spotify app and needs Spotify Premium. Keys and logins are stored encrypted on your PC only.

WHERE DO THE LYRICS COME FROM?

From LRCLIB, a free, open lyrics database with time-synced lines. Turn them on under SETTINGS → LYRICS.

WHY DO THE VIDEO TOOLS ASK FOR FFMPEG?

Video work uses ffmpeg, a free tool you can get from ffmpeg.org. Point the program to ffmpeg.exe once and it remembers.

DOES ANYTHING GET SENT ANYWHERE?

No. The launcher only talks to GitHub to download programs and check for updates. Your music, photos and settings stay on your PC.
